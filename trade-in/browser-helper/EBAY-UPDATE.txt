Play2Win Market Helper 1.1.9

Update each staff computer/profile manually:
1. Download the helper ZIP from Floor Pricing and extract it over the helper's
   permanent folder (the folder containing manifest.json).
2. Open chrome://extensions and click Reload on Play2Win Market Helper.
   Allow eBay search-page access if Chrome asks. Keep only one helper enabled.
3. Refresh the pricing tool. The header should say Connected - v1.1.9.
For first installation, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked instead.
Manual guide: https://play2wingames.com/trade-in/helper-setup.html
Connection checks: https://play2wingames.com/trade-in/helper.html
Privacy: https://play2wingames.com/trade-in/helper-privacy.html

New in 1.1.9: unreadable or failed sold-search pages get one fresh automatic
retry before a warning. Successful pages stay cached; failed retries keep
usable evidence and explain what still needs checking. Unknown shipping or
seller location alone does not trigger more tabs. Pricing and premiums are unchanged.

Since 1.1.8: reads the listing's "Located in" label on sold search cards and
original item shipping sections. The pricing tool excludes sellers outside
North America. Canada and other North American sales still include shipping,
regardless of the shipping amount. Missing location is shown for review.
Sales over six months old are excluded when at least three recent matching
sales exist. Older sales remain labeled evidence for scarce/slow-selling items;
an unfinished search cannot establish that exception.

Direct eBay sold search reads pages highest price plus shipping first.
It continues only while no eligible matching sale for the selected condition
has been found, then stops. Wrong games, editions, bundles, accepted offers
and wrong conditions cannot stop the search. Only Brand New evidence prices
New inventory; New (Other), Like New and open-box are used conditions.

The pricing tool decides eligibility and includes known shipping once. Changing
condition cancels the old search and reuses cached pages for the new condition.
One background search tab runs at a time, with page timeouts and automatic cleanup.
Failed, repeated, unreadable pages or the pagination safety limit require review.
A successful stop means a highest eligible match was found, not that the entire
sales history was read. Sign in to eBay in the same Chrome profile if needed.
The helper never fills credentials or solves challenges. Bulk audits/rechecks
never open helper tabs. Specific premiums and rounding stay in the pricing tool.

It reads only Play2Win trade-in, matching GameStop product, original eBay item
and eBay search pages. It never buys, bids, submits offers, changes carts or
messages sellers. Temporary tabs close automatically. Specific premiums,
rare-figure minimums and existing rounding remain in the pricing tool.
The website cannot reload an installed Chrome extension for you. This manual
installation does not auto-update and cannot run in the Codex in-app browser.
