Update the existing unpacked helper in Chrome at chrome://extensions:
click Reload on Play2Win's helper, approve eBay item-page access if Chrome asks,
then refresh the pricing tool. This folder now contains the complete update.
On another computer, replace the unpacked folder with the new downloaded ZIP
contents before clicking Reload.

If duplicate eBay product tabs are opening, switch the helper off first and
refresh every open pricing-tool tab to stop an old sweep. Reload this extension,
confirm Chrome shows version 1.1.5, turn it back on and refresh the pricing tool.
Close the unwanted duplicate product tabs. The website cannot reload an installed
Chrome extension for you. Version 1.1.2 rejects old automatic sweep requests;
the updated website refuses eBay checks through an older installed helper.

First installation: extract the ZIP, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer
mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the folder containing manifest.json.
Refresh https://play2wingames.com/trade-in/ in the same Chrome profile.
Each staff computer/profile needs its own installation. Remove the helper at
chrome://extensions. It cannot run inside the Codex in-app browser.

The helper reads matching GameStop product pages and eBay.com/itm/ item pages.
Targeted checks require version 1.1.5. The live tool checks only the sale driving
pricing when older than 90 days or at least $10 and 30% above matching Amazon.
The current price-driving sale and its next possible replacement check two at
a time, at most three total. A verified highest driver unlocks pricing without
waiting for its backup. Unknown stops further scheduling and flags Needs review. Add to list waits
while checking; typed reviewed prices remain available. Bulk rechecks and history
audits do not open eBay tabs. Manual Check selected sale remains available.
Checks retain the original-listing URL controls to avoid product-page redirects.
The helper expands See original listing, reads
the item's status/title/price, and close only those temporary tabs. It never
buys, bids, submits offers, messages sellers, changes carts, or solves challenges.
Archived original-item ENDED summary cards are excluded even without a price panel.
Only explicit sold status plus the original visible, non-struck USD price can
verify a sale. Blocked or unclear pages remain unverified, not confirmed sales.

Ended listings and accepted-offer/struck-through prices are excluded. A sold
price must be visible in USD and match the PriceCharting amount. Specific
premiums, rare-figure minimums and existing rounding still apply afterward.
Without the helper or if a page cannot be read, the check is inconclusive:
PriceCharting evidence stays eligible, labeled for manual review. A check takes
at most 12 seconds with this version per listing. Targeted checks temporarily hold Add to list.

Confirmed out-of-stock GameStop prices
are reference-only and do not set a minimum. Specific premiums still apply.
