Play2Win GameStop Stock Helper - Chrome - version 1.0.0

ONE-TIME INSTALL
1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
2. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select this folder
   (the folder containing manifest.json). If downloaded as ZIP, extract first.
3. Reload https://play2wingames.com/trade-in/ in the same Chrome profile.
   Sign in normally, choose Floor Pricing, and look up a game.

The helper automatically opens the matching GameStop product in a background
tab, reads the pre-owned cart and Ship to Home controls, then closes its tab.
The first check can take up to 25 seconds. Results are cached for 10 minutes.

ACCESS
Chrome will grant the helper access to Play2Win's /trade-in/ pages and GameStop
product pages. It only passes a product URL and stock result. It does not read
customer/trade fields, passwords, cookies, or browsing history, and never clicks
Add to Cart, buys anything, or sends data to another service. The GameStop page
loads in your normal browser session; local pickup may differ from online stock.

Confirmed Not Available = Out of stock online. A blocked, incomplete, wrong-item
or failed check = Check stock. The helper does not solve challenges or bypass
access controls. Confirmed out-of-stock removes GameStop's pricing minimum; specific premiums still apply.

This helper works only in the Chrome profile where installed. It cannot run in
the Codex in-app browser. Each staff computer/profile needs its own installation.
Remove it at chrome://extensions. To update, replace its files and press Reload
on its extension card, then reload the pricing tool.
