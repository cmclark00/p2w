Hardware prices

Cash prices from the Game Buying Guide for consoles, handhelds, controllers, and accessories. Missing cords and controllers are deductions (Settings tab).

Deductions

Staff add these to a line from its + Deduction menu. They come off the item value before the percentages. For games, if deductions are more than the value, the item goes in the $0.25 stack. Hardware bought for Parts is a flat parts price: deductions don't apply, and store credit is the same as cash. Tick Resurfacing for scratch deductions: the guide's flat prices are divided by 5 when resurfacing is needed.

Game buying guide rules

  • Don't buy: PC games and the dead games below.
  • Disc-only (Loose on a disc system): shitbox games $0.10 · under $10 $0.50 · $10–$20 $1.00 · over $20 priced normally.
  • Shitbox games in box (CIB or New) $0.25 when worth $20 or less.
  • Shitbox games: games needing an accessory, sports games (except the exceptions), and the listed titles. Staff can mark or unmark any game from its + Deduction menu.
  • Flat guide amounts are paid the same in cash and credit. One title or keyword per line in each box.

Flat-price items

Steering wheels and the like: anything whose name contains one of these phrases pays this amount in cash and credit, in any condition, with no deductions. One phrase per line; leave it empty to turn this off.

Floor price premiums

Floor Pricing adds this percentage on top of the automatic shelf price for games whose name contains the phrase. One phrase = percent per line, like Pokemon = 10. When several match, the longest phrase wins, so Pokemon Yellow = 50 raises one game and Pokemon X = 0 exempts one. Add @ system to limit a line to one system (as PriceCharting names it), like Pokemon @ Nintendo DS = 0; it beats the same phrase without a system. Typed prices never get a premium.
